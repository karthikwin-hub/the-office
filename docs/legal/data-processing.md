# Data processing terms

> **Draft for legal review.** These terms describe what we do with personal data on behalf of a business. They are written to fit India's DPDP Act now, and to be extended for the EU GDPR and Australia's Privacy Act later. Text in [square brackets] must be completed. Version 2026-11-06.

These terms form part of the [terms of service](/legal/terms) between [legal name of the company] ("we") and each business that opens an office ("the business").

## 1. Roles

- The business is the **data fiduciary** (controller) for the personal data in its office: its customers, suppliers, staff, and the people who write to it.
- We are its **data processor**. We process that data only to provide Agentic Office, on the business's documented instructions. Using the product (uploading, approving, connecting a channel, hiring an agent) is such an instruction.

## 2. What we process

| Category | Data subjects | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Books | Customers, suppliers, staff | Names, GSTIN, addresses, phone numbers, invoices, payments, salaries |
| Documents | People named in bills and invoices | PDFs and photos the business uploads or emails in |
| Customer conversations | The business's customers | WhatsApp, email and website chat messages, order numbers, contact details |
| Social media | People who comment on or message the business | Comments, reviews, direct messages |

## 3. Our commitments

We will:

1. process the data only on the business's instructions, and tell the business if we think an instruction breaks the law;
2. make sure our staff who can reach the data are bound to keep it confidential, and reach it only to support the business or keep the service safe;
3. keep each office apart from every other, encrypt data in transit and secrets at rest, and keep security records for 1 year;
4. use only the subprocessors on the [subprocessors list](/legal/subprocessors), give [30] days' notice before adding one, and hold each to terms at least as protective as these;
5. help the business answer its customers' requests to see, correct or erase their data;
6. tell the business **without undue delay**, and within [24 hours] of confirming it, about a breach that affects its data, with what we know and what we are doing, so it can meet its own duty to tell the Data Protection Board and the people affected;
7. not use the data to train AI models, and not sell or share it for anyone's own purposes;
8. at the end of the service, let the business export its data, and then erase it within 30 days of the office being deleted, except what the law requires us to keep;
9. give the business the information it reasonably needs to show it complies, and answer reasonable audit questions in writing.

## 4. The business's commitments

The business will:

- have a lawful basis to give us the data, and tell its customers about its use of Agentic Office, including the AI assistant (the [customer notice template](/legal/ai-notice) helps);
- not upload more personal data than it needs;
- choose a retention period for customer conversations that suits its own obligations.

## 5. Transfers outside India

Agentic Office runs on Cloudflare's global network, so data may be processed outside India. We do not transfer data to any country the Government of India has restricted. [For the EU: standard contractual clauses to be added.]
