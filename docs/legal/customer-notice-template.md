# Telling your customers about your AI assistant

> **Draft for legal review.** Version 2026-11-06. A template for businesses that use the Customer Service agent. Replace the words in [square brackets] with your own.

The law in many places, and Meta's rules for WhatsApp, say customers must know when they are talking to an AI, and must always be able to reach a person. The agent already says so in its first message of every conversation:

> You are chatting with [Business name]'s AI assistant. To reach a person at any time, contact [name and number] or reply PERSON.

Add a short notice where customers find you as well.

## For your website (near the chat, or on your privacy page)

> **Our chat assistant.** Questions in the chat on this site, on WhatsApp at [number] and by email at [address] are first answered by an AI assistant. It answers only from information we have written and from your own orders and invoices with us, and it shows you an order only after you confirm the phone number or email address on it. Refunds, complaints and anything it is unsure about come straight to us. Type PERSON at any time to reach [name].
>
> We keep chats for [12] months to answer you and improve our service, then delete them. Our software provider, Agentic Office, and its hosting provider Cloudflare process them for us; WhatsApp messages also pass through Meta. To see or delete your chats, write to [address].

## For your WhatsApp Business profile ("About" or description)

> Replies may come from our AI assistant. Reply PERSON to reach [name].

## For an email signature on replies

> This reply may have been sent by our AI assistant. Reply PERSON to reach a member of our team.
